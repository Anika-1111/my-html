Types of Flowers-

Flowers are an important part of nature. They come in different shapes, colors, and sizes.Flowers play a pivotal role in ecosystems across the globe. They are essential components of natural landscapes, contributing to the biodiversity that ensures healthy and resilient ecosystems. Flowers provide vital habitats and food sources for a variety of organisms, including insects, birds, and mammals. For instance, bee species rely heavily on flowers for nectar and pollen, which are crucial for their survival and, in turn, for the process of pollination which is vital for the reproduction of many plant species.

WHAT ARE FLOWERS?

A flower is the specialized reproductive structure found in flowering plants (angiosperms) responsible for producing seeds and ensuring the survival of the species.
